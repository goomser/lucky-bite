Lucky Bite

Pull the handle. Go eat. Free, no account, takes a minute to install.

On Android

Use Chrome for this (the round red, yellow and green icon).

  1. Open the link in ChromeTap the big button at the bottom of this page.
  2. Tap the three dots ⋮Top-right corner of Chrome.
  3. Tap “Install app”Some phones say “Add to Home screen” instead. Either works.
  4. Tap “Install”Lucky Bite appears with your other apps. Open it and allow location when asked.

On iPhone or iPad

This only works from Safari (the blue compass icon), not from Chrome or a text message preview.

  1. Open the link in SafariTap the big button at the bottom of this page. If it opens somewhere else, copy the link and paste it into Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button ⇧The square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen (top-right on iPad).
  3. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen”If you don’t see it, scroll the list further; it’s there.
  4. Tap “Add”Lucky Bite appears on your Home Screen. Open it and allow location when asked.
